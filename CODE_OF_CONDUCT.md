# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the person you are talking to is acting in good
faith, and give them the benefit of the doubt when their tone does not carry
over the internet.

## What is expected

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels. Someone asking a
  question you find obvious is someone learning.
- Give and accept criticism about the work rather than the person. "This leaks a
  file handle" is a review; "you don't understand RAII" is not.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, apologize, and move on.
  Nobody needs a post-mortem of your mistake.

## What is not

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
commit messages — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through
[private reporting](https://github.com/koneb71/gitup/security/advisories/new)
or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are
handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not follow this document,
and may block anyone whose behaviour is harmful or repeated. Enforcement aims to
be proportionate; a first misstep is usually a private word, not a ban.
